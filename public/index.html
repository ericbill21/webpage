Hi, I’m Eric!👋🏼
I’m a Master’s student at ETH Zurich, working on generative image models. I started out on the theory side with Christopher Morris studying graph neural networks, then explored magnitude preservation techniques with Cristian Perez (NeurIPS 2026, OPT). More recently, I’ve focused on multi-subject generation for text-to-image models, publishing JEDI (ICML 2025, PUT) and developing its theoretical extension, FOCUS with Enis Simsar and Thomas Hofmann.
