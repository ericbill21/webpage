Hi, I'm Eric!👋🏼

I’m a Master’s student at ETH Zurich, working on generative image models. I started out on the theory side with [Christopher Morris](https://chrsmrrs.github.io/) studying graph neural networks, then explored magnitude preservation techniques with [Cristian Perez](https://github.com/cristianpjensen) (NeurIPS 2026, OPT). More recently, I’ve focused on multi-subject generation for text-to-image models, publishing JEDI (ICML 2025, PUT) and developing its theoretical extension, FOCUS with [Enis Simsar](https://enis.dev/) and [Thomas Hofmann](https://da.inf.ethz.ch/people/ThomasHofmann/).
